# Anti-Slop Audit Report — `audit-001-2026-11-12`

- **Project:** Luma & Living Storefront Catalogue (`E:\Projects\storefront-catalogue`)
- **Mode:** Mode 1 (`DURING`) + Mode 2 (`AFTER` Audit & Verification)
- **Design Read:** Scandinavian minimalist furniture catalogue storefront for design-conscious home buyers, in a warm editorial architectural style, dial `ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`.

## Part 1: Findings Audited & Remediated from Google Stitch Reference Exports

1. **[HIGH — R-02] Em dash (`—`) in Stitch reference copy and page title**
   - **Reason:** Raw Stitch `code.html` used an em dash in `"New arrivals, restocks, and stories from the workshop — once a month, never more."` and `<title>Luma & Living — All Products</title>`.
   - **Resolution:** Replaced with a comma in `src/components/layout/Newsletter.tsx` and a pipe separator (`Luma & Living | All Products`) in `src/app/layout.tsx`. Verified via unit tests (`products.test.ts` and `catalog.test.ts`).

2. **[HIGH — R-25] Insufficient WCAG AA contrast on subtle text and sale tags in raw Stitch CSS**
   - **Reason:** Raw Stitch `#A39A8E` on `#FAF8F3` measured `2.57:1` (failing `4.5:1`), and `#D9534F` with `#FFFFFF` text measured `3.92:1`.
   - **Resolution:** Refined `--color-text-subtle` to `#696159` (`5.73:1` on `#FAF8F3`, `5.18:1` on `#F2ECE5`), `--color-text-muted` to `#6E685F` (`5.20:1`), and `--color-sale-tag` to `#B33932` (`5.92:1` with white text). All pairings verified via `contrast-check.py`.

3. **[HIGH — R-39] Blocked mobile viewport zoom and unlabeled newsletter input in raw Stitch HTML**
   - **Reason:** Mobile Stitch `code.html` included `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no` and relied on placeholder-only labeling for the email field.
   - **Resolution:** Configured Next.js `viewport` in `src/app/layout.tsx` without zoom restrictions and added a visible `<label htmlFor="newsletter-email">` in `src/components/layout/Newsletter.tsx`.

4. **[HIGH — R-24, R-26, R-39] Non-functional `<a href="#">` links and dead controls in raw Stitch HTML**
   - **Reason:** Navigation links, category pills, sort button, grid/list toggle, wishlist hearts, pagination links, and footer links were static `<a href="#">` or dead buttons.
   - **Resolution:** Replaced all actions with semantic `<button type="button">` elements wired to interactive state hooks (`useCatalog`, `useWishlist`, `useCart`), drawers, and editorial dialogs. Base CSS (`src/app/globals.css`) sets `cursor: pointer` on all enabled controls.

5. **[HIGH — R-27] Happy-path-only catalog grid without Empty, Loading, or Error states**
   - **Reason:** Raw Stitch export only rendered the ideal 12-item grid.
   - **Resolution:** Implemented perceivable Empty, Loading, and Error states in `src/components/products/ProductGrid.tsx` with clear cause descriptions and one-click recovery buttons (`Reset all filters`, `Show catalogue now`, `Reload Catalogue`).

6. **[HIGH — R-40] Unsized product images and lazy-loaded above-the-fold product cards**
   - **Reason:** Raw Stitch `<img>` elements omitted `width`/`height` attributes, and mobile Stitch lazy-loaded every card including row 1.
   - **Resolution:** Added `width={800} height={1000}` and `aspect-[4/5]` containers to all cards in `src/components/products/ProductCard.tsx`, with `loading="eager"` and `fetchPriority="high"` on the first 4 cards (`index < 4`).
